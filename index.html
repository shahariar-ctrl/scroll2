// about us

Built by students, for students

Gradely started as a late-night side project because doing NU's CGPA math by hand — across four years, ten grade bands and two different syllabuses — was genuinely not it. So we built a calculator that actually gets it right, and actually looks like something made in 2026.

Every department across BSc, BBA, BSS and BA Honours is covered, for both the 2024–2025 and 2013–2014 syllabuses, plus the Degree Pass course. Everything is grade-weighted and calculated live as you type — no sign-up, no spreadsheet, no cap.

Gradely is an independent educational project and isn't affiliated with National University Bangladesh — just students trying to make results day a little less stressful for everyone else.

// privacy policy

Your grades stay yours

Gradely doesn't ask for an account, an email, or a password — so there's nothing to leak in the first place. The grades you enter are saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device.

Nothing you type into the calculator is sent to a server, sold, or shared with anyone, including us. Clear your browser data or hit "Reset calculator" and it's gone for good.

// what we don't collect

No accounts, no email addresses, no phone numbers, no passwords. We don't run ad trackers, don't build user profiles, and don't sell or share data with advertisers — there simply isn't any to sell.

If you email us or message us through the Contact page, we only use that information to reply to you — never for marketing, and never passed on to third parties.

// how local storage works

Your grades are stored using your browser's built-in local storage, tied to this specific browser on this specific device. They're not synced to any account, so they won't automatically follow you to another phone, laptop, or browser.

Clearing your browser's site data, using private/incognito mode, or hitting "Reset calculator" will permanently erase that saved data — we have no copy to restore it from.

// cookies & analytics

Gradely doesn't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics scripts. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically generate basic technical logs (such as page requests and general traffic volume) for security and uptime purposes — these logs don't contain your grades or any personal identifiers.

If that ever changes — say, adding privacy-friendly, non-identifying analytics to see which features people actually use — this page will be updated to reflect it.

// third-party links

Occasional links (like the mailto link on Contact) point outside Gradely. We're not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites or services you reach through those links — check their own policies if you're unsure.

// who this is for

Gradely is built for current and prospective National University Bangladesh students, generally university-aged. It isn't designed or marketed for young children, and we don't knowingly collect any data from them — largely because we don't collect personal data from anyone in the first place.
